## 1. Required Riot disclaimers

Two Riot documents each prescribe a notice. Show both.

### 1a. Developer policy boilerplate (required)

Source: https://developer.riotgames.com/policies/general — "You must post the following legal boilerplate to your product in a location that is readily visible to players"

> Mayhem Lab isn't endorsed by Riot Games and doesn't reflect the views or opinions of Riot Games or anyone officially involved in producing or managing Riot Games properties. Riot Games, and all associated properties are trademarks or registered trademarks of Riot Games, Inc.

### 1b. Legal Jibber Jabber notice (requested when a project is shared)

Source: https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal — "please conspicuously include the following notice (e.g., on your Project's website)"

> Mayhem Lab was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.

### Placement

- Site footer on every page (covers "readily visible to players" and "conspicuously").
- Repeat on an About/Legal page with the attribution lines below.
- For a future Overwolf app: in the app's settings/about window and store listing.

### Related rules

- Do not use Riot logos or trademarks as branding. "You may not use any of our logos or trademarks anywhere in your Project" (Legal Jibber Jabber). The product name, domain and social handles must not contain Riot names.
- The product must not "closely resemble Riot's games or products in style or function" (general policy). On Overwolf: "Your overlay should never look like it's part of the official Riot game UI."

Notes
- **Wiki share-alike:** copying or adapting wiki prose places that derived text under CC BY-SA 3.0. To keep Mayhem Lab's explanations freely licensable on our own terms, write them independently and use the wiki for fact-checking only. Facts themselves (numbers, names) come from Riot data, not the wiki.
- **arammayhem.com:** its licence covers the operator's own compilation only and says nothing about Riot's position. The operator is not identified. Using it is optional and not currently planned.
- **No third-party stat sites** (op.gg, u.gg, etc.) are used as data sources. Their terms were not reviewed.
